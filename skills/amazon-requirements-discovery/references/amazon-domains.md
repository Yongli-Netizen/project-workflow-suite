# Amazon domain discovery prompts

Load only the relevant domain sections. These are decision prompts, not a questionnaire; ask only what materially changes the solution.

## Shared context

- Marketplace(s), seller/vendor model, brand ownership, stores/accounts, locale, currency, and tax basis.
- User roles and actual operating workflow, frequency/volume, current tools, approval chain, and acceptable automation level.
- Available SP-API, Ads API, Brand Analytics/ABA, Seller Central exports, ERP, procurement, logistics, cost, Keepa, or other licensed data; sample quality and access permissions.
- Read/write boundaries, spend or inventory limits, audit trail, rollback, retention, privacy, and regulatory constraints.

## Product and competitor research

Clarify category and exclusions, target margin and demand measures, seasonality, market/keyword coverage, competitor definition, data freshness, sourcing constraints, IP/compliance risk, and how a decision will be judged.

## Listing and keyword optimization

Clarify ASIN/SKU scope, source language and marketplaces, Brand Registry/A+ access, keyword sources, prohibited claims, category style constraints, approval/publishing authority, experiment method, and target metrics such as CTR or conversion.

## PPC advertising

Clarify portfolios/campaign types, reporting grain and attribution window, optimization objective, budget and bid limits, negative-keyword rules, change approval, API write access, rollback, and success measures such as ACOS, TACOS, ROAS, or incremental profit.

## Reviews, returns, and customer signals

Clarify accessible review/return/VOC sources, time range, language, variation grouping, taxonomy needs, PII handling, sampling bias, alert thresholds, and whether outputs are insights, tickets, or automated actions.

## Inventory, replenishment, and profitability

Clarify SKU and location scope, lead times, MOQ/case packs, inbound and transfer states, demand horizon, safety stock/service level, stockout/overstock cost, landed-cost components, fees/tax/currency, forecast overrides, and purchase-order authority.

## Compliance and multi-store operations

Clarify product/category/market regulations, evidence source and responsible reviewer, restricted claims/materials, account/role isolation, approval separation, audit and retention needs, cross-store normalization, and escalation behavior. Treat generated compliance output as decision support unless qualified authority and workflow say otherwise.

## Reporting and automation

Clarify audience, decisions supported, metrics and formulas, timezone and refresh SLA, delivery channel, failure notification, backfill/reconciliation, idempotency, rate limits, manual override, and which actions must remain human-approved.
